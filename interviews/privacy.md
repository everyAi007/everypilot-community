# Privacy Policy

_Effective 6 November 2026. Your progress and your own answers stay on your phone. If you sign in, we keep only what runs your account and plan._

This Privacy Policy explains how the everyPilot: Airline Interviews app handles information, and how Eyinsan Odugba (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles the little information we do receive.

## In short

- Your progress (questions answered, your streak, your target airline and interview date, your own answers, questions you add, mock results and settings) is stored on your device. We don’t receive it.
- Signing in is optional until you buy a plan. If you sign in (Apple, Google or an email code), our server keeps an account id, how you signed in, your email address if you used email, your plan, and which free and paid questions and answers your account has opened. That is what runs your plan and stops the bank being copied.
- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of personal information.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time (Me › Account › Delete account).
- Community questions (optional): if you share a question you added, our server keeps the question, its category and airline, your answer and the date, shown to other pilots under a pen name. The post isn’t stored with your account id (only a scrambled code that lets you edit or delete it). Deleting the question or your account removes it.
- Reading files and AI answers (not switched on yet): when we turn them on, a PDF, photo or Word file you send, or a question you ask an AI answer for, will go to our server and Google’s Gemini AI service, as described under “My questions” below.
- When we add sync, this policy will say exactly what is sent, where and why, and the app will ask you to agree before it starts.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores on your device: your role (for example cadet or first officer) and rule set (FAA, EASA or ICAO), your target airline and interview date, which questions you have answered and how, when they are due again, your daily study log and streak, your own written answers, mock interview results, and your settings. Your device’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include this under your own account. You can erase it all in Me › Privacy & security › Erase everything on this device.

## Permissions the app asks for

- Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint (optional app lock): your device checks it itself; the app only learns whether the check passed.
- Age range (iPhone): where a US state’s app-store law applies, the app asks Apple for your age range (never your birthday). It is used only to check the minimum age, stays on your device, and is never sent to us.
- Notifications (optional): daily study reminders are scheduled on your phone. If you’re signed in and reminders are on, we also keep your phone’s push token, time zone and reminder hour so we can send at most two kinds of note: one if you haven’t opened the app for a week (no more than 3 in a row), and a heads-up before a 3-month pass ends. Turning reminders off, signing out or deleting your account removes them from our server. Expo (our app platform) relays the notification to Apple or Google to deliver it; the note itself contains no question text or personal details.
- Microphone and speech recognition (optional, only when you tap “Answer out loud”): your spoken answer is turned into text to time it and count filler words. Where your phone supports it this happens on the phone; otherwise your phone’s own speech service (Apple or Google) processes the audio under their terms. We never receive the audio or the text unless you save it as your answer, which stays on your phone.
- Files (optional): if you choose a logbook export to find stories, or a text, CSV or JSON file of interview questions, the app reads it on your phone and nothing from it is sent to us. A PDF, photo or Word file you choose for My questions is sent to be read (see “My questions” below).
- Camera and photos (optional, only when you tap Camera or Photos in My questions): the app opens only the photos you pick or take, and sends them to be read for questions. It doesn’t browse your photo library.

## Your account (if you sign in)

- Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us a private user id and, if you choose to share it, your email (which can be an Apple relay address). Sign in with Google: Google gives us a user id; we don’t keep your Google profile. Email: we send a one-time 6-digit code to the address you type; we keep the address as your sign-in.
- We keep: an account id, how you signed in, your email address if you used email sign-in, your plan and its end date, the App Store or Google Play transaction id (to confirm the purchase and stop it being used by another account), your free questions, a count of answers opened each day, and (only with reminders on) your phone’s push token, time zone and reminder hour.
- Why: to run the account and the plan you bought (contract), and to protect our content from bulk copying and fraud (legitimate interest).
- How long: while your account exists. When you delete it, we delete the account, sign-ins, plan, free questions and counts within 30 days; we keep the store transaction id, detached from you, for up to 7 years for tax and fraud records. A subscription is cancelled in your App Store or Google Play settings, not by deleting the account.
- Each answer is shown with a faint mark made from a scrambled version of your account id, so a leaked screenshot can be traced to an account. The mark doesn’t contain your name or email.
- Questions for your plan are downloaded to your phone and stored encrypted with a key that stays on your phone.
- Interview reports you send: the airline, date, how the day ran, the questions (not the ones you mark as about your background), your outcome and class date, your notes and whether the app helped. Emails, phone numbers and names after titles are removed automatically. Other pilots see the report under a random label such as “Pilot 7KQ2”, never your name or account. Reports are kept to keep the question bank accurate; they are stored without your account id, so deleting your account doesn’t identify them, and you can ask us to remove one.
- Study buddy (optional): the nickname you choose, your current streak and whether you studied today are shared with the one pilot you pair with.
- School codes and referrals: if you use a school code, we record the seat against your account (your school can ask us how many seats are used, not what you study). If you use a friend’s code, we record that link so they can get their free month.

## Community questions

- What is sent: the question you add with sharing on, its category, the airline you typed, and your answer to it (and later edits to that answer). Emails, phone numbers and names after titles are removed automatically.
- Who sees it: every pilot using the app, under your pen name (made from a scrambled version of your account id, the same on all your posts so other pilots can hide them). Never your name or email.
- What we keep: the post, its date, how many pilots reported it (as scrambled codes), and our review status. We use the questions to grow our bank, with our own answers.
- How long: until you delete the post, the question, or your account, or we remove it. Questions already added to our bank stay there, in our own words, without your answer or pen name.
- Why: to share what you asked to share (contract), and to improve the question bank (legitimate interest).
- Hidden pilots are saved on your phone only.

## My questions: reading files and AI answers (not switched on yet)

These features are built but not yet turned on. When they are, this is how they will work, and the app will show the buttons for them.

- What is sent: the PDF, photo or Word file you choose (up to 8 MB), and the airline you typed, if any. For an AI answer, only the question’s text and category, never the file.
- Who processes it: our server (DigitalOcean, United States) and Google’s Gemini API, which reads the file or writes the answer for us as a service provider. We send it to Google only through the paid service, where Google doesn’t use it to improve its products.
- What we keep: never the file. We keep each new question’s text (names, emails and phone numbers removed), its category and airline, how many times it was sent and when, and any AI answer written for it, without your account id. We use them to review and grow the question bank, and they may be shown to other pilots. Answers written in your file are returned to your phone and not kept.
- Limits: a few files and AI answers a day, counted against your account and reset daily.
- Only send what you’re free to share. If an airline asked you not to share its questions, don’t send them.
- Why: to provide the feature you asked for (contract), and to improve the question bank (legitimate interest). You can ask us to remove a question you sent.

## Security

Your progress is protected by your device’s encryption and passcode. Connections to our server use HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage (Keychain / Keystore), and we never store passwords because there aren’t any. You can add an app lock and hide the app’s content in the app switcher. Saved data is checked field by field before use. No method of storage is completely secure; please protect your device with a passcode.
