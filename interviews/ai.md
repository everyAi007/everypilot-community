# Study Content & AI Notice

_Where the questions and answers come from, and how to use them._

## The questions

- Collected from interview reports that pilots posted publicly (forums, write-ups, prep sites’ free pages and shared study decks), then cleaned, merged and rewritten in our own words.
- “Asked at N airlines” counts the airlines where a question was reported; the date is the latest report we found.
- Content behind logins or paywalls was not used.

## The answers

- Written by us from named sources (regulations, official handbooks, accident reports and pilot competency frameworks). Each answer lists its sources.
- Some answers are drafted with AI help, then checked against their sources. Each answer shows its status.
- Regulations differ between authorities and change over time, and airlines have their own procedures. Your current regulations, aircraft manuals and company manuals always take priority.
- They are study aids, not flight instruction and not official guidance from any airline or regulator.

## Questions you add

- Community answers are written by other pilots, not by us, and are not checked. Questions we add to our bank from the community get our own answers, written from named sources.
- When AI answers are switched on: if you add a question that isn’t in our bank, you can get an AI draft answer in a few seconds. It is written by an AI model (Google Gemini) to the same rules as our answers, but no pilot has checked it yet, and it is marked “AI draft”. Check it against your manuals.
- Questions read from your files are found by AI and can contain mistakes: look over the list before you add it.

## Airlines

Airline names identify the airlines whose interviews are described. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any airline. Interview processes change; profiles show the date of the latest report.

## Found a mistake?

Tap “Report a problem” on any answer, or email us. We fix confirmed errors and note the change.
