# Delete your everyPilot: Airline Interviews account

_everyPilot: Airline Interviews, published by Eyinsan Odugba._

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open the app and go to **Me › Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away from the app's side, and from our server within 30 days.

## By email

If you no longer have the app, email **everyai007@gmail.com** from the address you signed in with (or tell us how you signed in: Apple, Google or email code). Write "Delete my Interviews account". We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account id and sign-ins (Apple, Google or email)
- Your plan and its end date
- Your free questions and daily counts
- Your reminder (push) details, if you turned reminders on

## What is kept

- The App Store or Google Play transaction id, detached from you, for up to 7 years for tax and fraud records.
- Interview reports you sent are stored without your account id, so they no longer link to you. You can ask us to remove a report.

Your progress and your own answers are stored only on your phone. Erase them in **Me › Privacy & security › Erase everything on this device**, or by uninstalling the app.

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
